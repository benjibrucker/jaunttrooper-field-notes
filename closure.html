Plain-language technical hint

creature + 21 means a field inside one creature’s stored state. Bit 6 of that byte and bit 7 of byte 20 are flags. They do not tell us which enemy you saw.

For the current creature, after movement:
  check that the flag in byte 21, bit 6, is clear
  check that the flag in byte 20, bit 7, is set
  earlier perception and random-choice conditions also matter
  the full perception/random gate is still only partially recovered

The useful player question is: Which creature seemed to mutter, what did you do immediately before it, and how far away was it? You do not need to interpret offsets or flags to answer.
